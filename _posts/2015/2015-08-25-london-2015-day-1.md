We started at the [British Library](https://www.bl.uk/). The Magna Carta exhibit had Jefferson's copy of the Declaration of Independence and a copy of the Bill of Rights. In the permanent collection, I saw Shakespeare's First Folio, a Gutenberg Bible, and part of the fifth-century Codex Alexandrinus, open to the end of Luke and beginning of John. The Beatles display sent me down a small “Help!” rabbit hole.

Then it was a black cab to [Temple Church](https://www.templechurch.com/), with its connection to King John and Magna Carta. The [Charles Dickens Museum](https://dickensmuseum.com/) was next; Dickens lived in the house for about three years. We also stopped at [Persephone Books](https://persephonebooks.co.uk/), with its uniform gray covers and shelves full of women writers, and wandered around [Covent Garden](https://www.coventgarden.london/). Ksenia found the Moomin shop; I found a pint.

Dinner at [The Jones Family Project](https://www.opentable.co.uk/r/the-jones-family-project) was very good. I had a rib-eye with a steak tartare appetizer and a Delmonico 18/27 cocktail.
