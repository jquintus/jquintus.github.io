At [The Cartoon Museum](https://www.cartoonmuseum.org/), I found lots of Alice. Then we stopped at [Tea and Tattle](https://www.inyourpocket.com/england/london/venues/tea-tattle) for a peaceful break. A great scone, good tea, a good sandwich, and a nice atmosphere.

We headed back to [Covent Garden](https://www.coventgarden.london/) and watched the street performers. I remembered the showmanship as much as the acts themselves.
