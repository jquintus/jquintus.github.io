We took the train from Paddington to Oxford. At the [Bodleian Library](https://www.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/), the gift shop had all the things: bookmarks, coasters, pins, bags, Christmas ornaments, hanging signs. The notebook says “Happy Wife.”

The [History of Science Museum](https://hsm.ox.ac.uk/) had Einstein's blackboard, astrolabes, quadrants, scopes, and Marconi's radio equipment. On a walking tour we saw [Wadham College](https://www.wadham.ox.ac.uk/) and learned about the group that helped give rise to the Royal Society; Christopher Wren came up too. A full day of books, instruments, and Oxford history before the train back to London.
