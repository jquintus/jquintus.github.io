We visited the vet with James and Lilly, then went to [Tate Modern](https://www.tate.org.uk/visit/tate-modern). I jotted down Picasso, Dalí, Judd's copper box and blue shelves, Warhol's self-portrait and Marilyn Monroe, Penone's *Tree in London*, and Hiorns's copper crystals on an engine block. [Waterloo Station](https://www.networkrail.co.uk/communities/passengers/our-stations/london-waterloo/) was large, with no sunset that I could see.

We saw *As You Like It* at [Shakespeare's Globe](https://www.shakespearesglobe.com/). Dinner was at [Katzenjammers](https://www.london-se1.co.uk/restaurants/info/727/katzenjammers), the German beer hall near Borough Market. The bratwurst and the rest of the German food were great.
