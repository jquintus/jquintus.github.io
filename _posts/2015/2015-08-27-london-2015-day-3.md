We watched a stranger use a giant touch screen in the [National Gallery](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/) and went across Trafalgar Square to [St Martin-in-the-Fields](https://www.stmartin-in-the-fields.org/). I spent some time looking through bookshops on Charing Cross Road, and lunch at [Princi](https://www.timeout.com/london/restaurants/princi) was meat lasagna. It wasn't as good as I remembered, though still good. Ksenia and I split a strawberry dessert; I had to stay alert to get my share of the strawberry. We'd eaten there with her cousin on a previous London visit.

The [Polish Institute and Sikorski Museum](https://pism.org.uk/visits/) was wonderful: three floors, a personal tour, and Polish government memorabilia, including a commission signed by Napoleon in 1812 and Sikorski's desk. The visit to [Benjamin Franklin House](https://benjaminfranklinhouse.org/) was a walking play through the house. Basic information, but worth going.

Around the corner, [MOC Kitchen](https://www.timeout.com/london/restaurants/moc-kitchen) served a delicious Vietnamese bánh mì with braised pork. We also went to [Waterstones](https://www.waterstones.com/), where I noted Andrew Smith and Michael Grant.
