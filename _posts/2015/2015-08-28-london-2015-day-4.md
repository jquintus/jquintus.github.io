We toured London with Dickie, a Beefeater guide. I wrote down the Battle of Hastings in 1066 and a story about the executioner. Dickie had plenty of stories. At [Tower Bridge](https://www.towerbridge.org.uk/), walking across the glass floor was amazing and a little unnerving.

At [Ye Olde Cheshire Cheese](https://ye-olde-cheshire-cheese.co.uk/), we had Samuel Smith Taddy Lager and cider; Ksenia had a steak and ale pie. A famous old pub and a good place to sit down after the tour.
