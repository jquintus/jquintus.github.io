We walked [Portobello Road Market](https://visitportobello.com/) and browsed a Scottish Highland shop, a map and print shop, and other stalls. At [Coffee Plant](https://www.coffeeplant.uk/) I had an espresso and an iced chai.

At [The Duke of Wellington](https://www.thedukeofwellingtonpub.com/), I had wild boar sausage. The lemonade was not good, but the meal was very good. The waitress explained that the French word for zucchini is *courgette*, which was useful to know. Later we saw *45 Years*: a very British movie, and okay. We went home early for some rest and laundry.
