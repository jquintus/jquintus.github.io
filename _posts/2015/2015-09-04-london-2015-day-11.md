We visited a wolf sanctuary and saw lots of wolves. There were kites overhead too, birds I had thought were no longer common. Wolves are awesome.

Dinner at [The Pig & Butcher](https://thepigandbutcher.co.uk/) was great. We had a cheese plate and rolled pork belly. A very good way to end the trip.
