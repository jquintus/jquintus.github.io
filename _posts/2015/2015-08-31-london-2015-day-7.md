We climbed the steep path into [Highgate Cemetery](https://highgatecemetery.org/). At Karl Marx's grave, Ksenia said, “Let's get Marx out of the way,” and I told her she sounded like Lenin. We also found the graves of Douglas Adams and George Eliot. The inscription on Eliot's stone sent us into another round of jokes about existence.

We had dinner with Gibson. Good food, and I like Gibbo.
