## Animal Tallies

| Animal | Today | Trip total |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Wolves | 5 | 5 |
| Bears | 0 | 1 |
| Coyotes | 0 | 0 |
| Moose | 0 | 0 |

We woke up to wolf howling, then drove to Gardiner. The drive prompted a few observations:

“Is the speed limit really 80?”

“It's so pretty.” This was code for “Josh, take a picture.”

“I can see why it's called Big Sky.”

Lunch was at [Iron Horse Bar & Grill][iron-horse]. I had a pulled pork sandwich. The food was so-so, but the view and patio were nice. It was too hot to sit outside, though.

Then: Yellowstone! The guy at the entrance was from Flushing.

We walked up to the top of [Mammoth Hot Springs][mammoth], stopped at [Roaring Mountain][roaring-mountain], and saw [Obsidian Cliff][obsidian-cliff] and the [Roosevelt Arch][roosevelt-arch]. Roaring Mountain offered two gas vents and no roars.

Dinner at [Antler Pub & Grill][antler] was a chicken sandwich. It was OK. We checked into [Yellowstone Village Inn & Suites][yellowstone-village], packed for our wolf guide in the morning, and went to bed early.

One more sticker brought my running total to 15. No new T-shirts today.

Part of our June 2024 Yellowstone and Grand Teton trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2024/2024-06-22-yellowstone-trip-summary %}).
