It was 35°F when we woke up, and it snowed throughout the day. We skipped our morning boat ride down the Snake River. It was just too cold; we would have been miserable.

Instead we went to [Mormon Row][mormon-row] for farmhouses and pictures. I was surprised by how many wire nails were used in the buildings. I had expected more cut nails. Ksenia made friends with another photographer. There were ground squirrels.

Lunch was pizza at [Dornan’s Pizza & Pasta Co.][dornans]. It was packed. The food was OK, but the view was great.

At the [National Museum of Wildlife Art][wildlife-art], we looked at animal pictures, including William Holbrook Beard’s “So You Wanna Get Married, Eh?”

Then [Schwabacher Landing][schwabacher]: pretty views, ducks, and Ksenia abandoning me for a squirrel. It warmed up, and the snow finally stopped.

We made it to [Teton Thai][teton-thai] for dinner this time. Small place, good food.

Part of our June 2024 Yellowstone and Grand Teton trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2024/2024-06-22-yellowstone-trip-summary %}).
