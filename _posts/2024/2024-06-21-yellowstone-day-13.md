Travel day.

Breakfast at the [RSVP Motel][rsvp] was surprisingly good. Would recommend. The hotel’s café is [Farmer’s Daughters][farmers-daughters]; my notebook records the meal as “breakfast at RSVP.”

We checked out at 10:40 and left our extra snacks, water, bug spray, and other supplies with the front desk. They were excited to get them.

Around 11 we wandered Bozeman’s Main Street. More stickers. I knocked over a display at a toy store.

Our flight to Minneapolis was delayed by 40 minutes, leaving us about 30 minutes to make the connection. That flight was delayed, too. We finally made it to New York at 1:30 in the morning.

Part of our June 2024 Yellowstone and Grand Teton trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2024/2024-06-22-yellowstone-trip-summary %}).
