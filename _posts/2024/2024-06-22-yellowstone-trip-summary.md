Ksenia and I spent June 9–21, 2024 in Bozeman, Gardiner, Yellowstone, and the Grand Tetons. We went for wolves and bears. We also got geysers, waffles, and a snowstorm. In June.

## Parks, Landmarks, and Other Stops

### Yellowstone National Park

We entered [Yellowstone][yellowstone] from Gardiner, spent several days on wildlife and thermal features, and came back through on our way to Bozeman.

| Stop | Highlights |
| --- | --- |
| [Lamar Valley][lamar-valley] and the northern range | Wolves, bears, bison, elk, pronghorns, a distant mountain goat, and a grouse attacking a car. The Junction Butte wolf pack was a highlight. |
| [Mammoth Hot Springs][mammoth] | A short hike up to the top. |
| [Roaring Mountain][roaring-mountain] | Two gas vents. No roars. |
| [Obsidian Cliff][obsidian-cliff] and [Roosevelt Arch][roosevelt-arch] | Stops on our first day in the park. |
| [Artist Point][artist-point] | The Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone and Lower Falls. Pretty reds, pinks, and oranges in the rock. |
| [Fountain Paint Pot][fountain-paint-pot] | Paint pots, springs, and geysers. Jet Geyser went off as we walked by. |
| [Midway Geyser Basin][midway] and [Grand Prismatic Spring][grand-prismatic] | Colorful and windy. The roughly 1.6-mile walk to the overlook was worth it for the view from above. |
| [Upper Geyser Basin and Old Faithful][old-faithful] | Two eruptions with lucky timing. The second was longer and taller than the first. |

### Grand Teton National Park and Jackson Hole

[Grand Teton National Park][grand-teton] brought moose, more bears, repeated attempts at the right light, and snow in June.

| Stop | Highlights |
| --- | --- |
| [Mormon Row][mormon-row] | Farmhouses, ground squirrels, and other photographers. It took four visits to get the light just right. |
| [Schwabacher Landing][schwabacher] | Pretty views and ducks. Ksenia abandoned me for a squirrel. |
| [Oxbow Bend][oxbow] | Nice pictures, including one a passing visitor stopped to show me. |
| [Mount Moran Turnout][moran] | A late-afternoon photo stop. |
| [Jackson Hole aerial tram][tram] | Up to roughly 10,000 feet, nice people, and waffles at [Corbet’s Cabin][corbets]. Better than they needed to be. |

We skipped our planned Snake River boat ride because it was 35°F and snowing. We would have been miserable.

### Animal Sanctuaries and Wildlife Tours

| Place or tour | What I thought |
| --- | --- |
| [Howlers Inn][howlers-inn] | Waking up to wolf howling, watching the same five sanctuary wolves, and casual wolf watching before bed. |
| [Montana Grizzly Encounter][grizzly-encounter] | An hour watching Max the bear and chatting with Wyatt, the guide. |
| [Yellowstone Wolf Tracker][wolf-tracker] | Highly recommend. Our guide Michael brought scopes, food, and a plan, and knew the individual animals we were watching. Worth the 5 AM pickup. |
| [Grizzly & Wolf Discovery Center][discovery-center] | Bears, wolves, otters, and birds. Wade the otter sucked on his tail like a kid sucking their thumb. |
| [BrushBuck Wildlife Tours][brushbuck] | Two moose sightings, elk, and pronghorns. A cold outing, but we saw a lot of the park. |

## About the Tallies and Ratings

The totals include animals at sanctuaries and discovery centers. I didn't add the five wolves at [Howlers Inn][howlers-inn] again every morning, but I wasn't perfectly consistent about repeat sightings. Treat these as trip tallies, rather than a count of unique wild animals.

We also saw bison, elk, pronghorns, a mountain goat, grouse, otters, eagles, ducks, and plenty of squirrels. I didn't keep totals for those. A zero in a daily table means we didn't see any of that animal that day; June 15 separates new sightings from repeats.

Five stars means a standout; four means I'd recommend it; three means OK or mixed. I haven't rated every meal. These are my impressions from June 2024.
