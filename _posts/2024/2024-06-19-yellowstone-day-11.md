We got to sleep in today!!!

Then we took the [Jackson Hole aerial tram][tram] up to roughly 10,000 feet above sea level. There were waffles at the top, at [Corbet’s Cabin][corbets]. Better than they needed to be. We met nice people, too.

Back to [Mormon Row][mormon-row] at 2:15 for more pictures, then [Oxbow Bend][oxbow]. Nice views. A lady stopped to show me her own picture of the view. Ksenia spotted a pronghorn along the way.

Between 4 and 4:20, we saw two grizzlies. As usual, people pulled over beside the road helped us find them. At 4:35 we stopped at [Moran’s turnout][moran].

Dinner was back at the [Mangy Moose][mangy-moose]: a burger for Ksenia and Arctic char for me. Then home to pack.

Two bears and one sticker. Not a bad day.

Part of our June 2024 Yellowstone and Grand Teton trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2024/2024-06-22-yellowstone-trip-summary %}).
