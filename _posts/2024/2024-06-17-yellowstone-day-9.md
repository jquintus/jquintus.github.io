## Animal Tallies

| Animal | Today | Trip total |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wolves | 0 | 28 |
| Bears | 0 | 29 |
| Coyotes | 0 | 5 |
| Moose | 2 | 3 |

We went out with [BrushBuck Wildlife Tours][brushbuck] and saw moose at 6:45 and 7:05. We learned that “moose” means “twig eater.” There were elk and pronghorns, too, although I didn’t keep counts for those.

The tour was cold. We didn’t see much else in the way of animals, but we did get to see a lot of the park.

Around 11 we stopped at [Café Genevieve][genevieve] in Jackson. I had chicken and waffles; Ksenia had a waffle. They served me cold tea, then replaced it quickly.

We went shopping for warmer clothes. I don’t like Jackson. You can feel the richie-riches in the air.

Then: a snowstorm. In June. The wind was rocking the car while it was parked.

We tried to go to [Teton Thai][teton-thai], but the wait would have meant standing outside in the snow. We went back to the [Mangy Moose][mangy-moose] instead. Elk and vegetable soup, fried pie, ragout for Ksenia, and ribs for me.

Part of our June 2024 Yellowstone and Grand Teton trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2024/2024-06-22-yellowstone-trip-summary %}).
