## Animal Tallies

| Animal | Today | Trip total |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wolves | 0 | 28 |
| Bears | 0 | 31 |
| Coyotes | 1 | 6 |
| Moose | 0 | 3 |

At 8:45 we made our fourth trip to [Mormon Row][mormon-row]. Finally, the light was right for pictures. Not too cloudy or rainy. Not too backlit. Just right.

We spent about half an hour on a “false bear” sighting. Rocks had already earned that nickname earlier in the trip. No addition to the bear tally.

At 11:05, our triumphant return to Yellowstone. We stopped at the post office around 2:30, met a really nice postal worker, and bought Ansel Adams stamps. Ten minutes later, a coyote appeared as we pulled out.

By about 4 we were at the [RSVP Motel][rsvp] in Bozeman. So cute. Highly recommend.

Dinner around 6, followed by a migraine. I suffered until going to bed at 7:15. Not the evening I would have picked.

A reminder to myself from today’s notes: look up frost heave.

Part of our June 2024 Yellowstone and Grand Teton trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2024/2024-06-22-yellowstone-trip-summary %}).
