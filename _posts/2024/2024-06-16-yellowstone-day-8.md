## Animal Tallies

We stopped at [Ernie’s Bakery & Deli][ernies] on Firehole Street to order boxed lunches: roast beef for me and ham for Ksenia. It looked like a good breakfast spot, although we were there for sandwiches. The roast beef could have been better.

At 9:51 we saw two coyotes. The first was beside the road with a fresh deer kill. The second was a little farther along, walking in the road.

We reached [Upper Geyser Basin][upper-geyser] around 10:45 and managed to see [Old Faithful][old-faithful] twice. For the first eruption, we got there just in time to get a good “seat,” by which I mean we stood on a fallen log. It lasted about two minutes and didn’t go all that high.

Our timing worked again after a loop around the basin. We finished the walk, had a good view, and caught a second eruption around 11:40. This one was longer and taller than the first.

After our picnic lunch from [Ernie’s][ernies], we drove toward the Tetons. Ksenia spotted a wolf after we stopped where other people had pulled over. We also saw a moose along Moose-Wilson Road. My note says “in river fishing?” The question mark is doing some work there.

Dinner was at the [Mangy Moose][mangy-moose] in Teton Village. We both had elk stroganoff. The elk was tender, and the wine stood out pleasantly.

Part of our June 2024 Yellowstone and Grand Teton trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2024/2024-06-22-yellowstone-trip-summary %}).
