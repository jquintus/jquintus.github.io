## In which we shall Frolic w/ Wolves + Bears

*(Oh, My!)*

Our trip began with a 3:30 AM alarm.

We were out the door by 4:45, flew through Minneapolis–Saint Paul, and landed in Bozeman. We picked up our rental, a Jeep Compass, then headed to Target for the supplies we'd ordered ahead. Somewhere along the way I lost my sunglasses, so another shopping stop supplied replacements and a Leatherman.

Dinner was [Five Guys][five-guys]. Then we checked into [Howlers Inn][howlers-inn], a bed-and-breakfast that is also a wolf sanctuary. Five wolves on day one seemed like a pretty good start.

After plenty of wolf watching, we went to bed tuckered out. The trip's sticker collection was already underway: two acquired, two total.

*Tally note: A zero means we didn't see any of that animal that day. The totals include sanctuary animals and some repeat sightings; they aren't counts of unique wild animals.*
