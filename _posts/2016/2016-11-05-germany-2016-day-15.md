We took a lazy last day in Munich: walked to the State Library, then to the [Siegestor](https://www.muenchen.de/en/sights/siegestor), Munich's triumphal arch, and on to a cemetery. We walked through [Viktualienmarkt](https://www.munich.travel/en/pois/markets-festivals/viktualienmarkt), stopping for hot chocolate at [Chocolaterie Beluga](https://chocolateriebeluga.de/kontakt/) and tea and cake at [Café Piemonte](https://www.mux.de/Cafe-Piemonte-Petersplatz).
