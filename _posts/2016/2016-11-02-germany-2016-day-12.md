We slept in and had breakfast at our leisure. At the [Pinakothek der Moderne](https://www.pinakothek-der-moderne.de/en/), we saw Dalí, Picasso, and Warhol. It was not as good as the Tate in London. The [Neue Pinakothek](https://www.pinakothek.de/en/visit/neue-pinakothek) had Manet, Monet, Cross, Klimt, and Van Gogh.

We found an English bookstore and had an early dinner at [Spatenhaus](https://www.kuffler.de/en/spatenhaus/). Ksenia also got yelled at because her purse was too big for the museum.
