We toured [Berlin-Hohenschönhausen Memorial](https://www.stiftung-hsh.de/en/), the former Stasi prison. The solitary confinement, years of “temporary” detention before trial, and constant interrogations were terrifying. The sensory-deprivation room especially got to me; they called it the “calming room.”

We went back by [Checkpoint Charlie](https://www.visitberlin.de/en/checkpoint-charlie), the Wall, and the [Memorial to the Murdered Jews of Europe](https://www.stiftung-denkmal.de/en/memorials/the-memorial-to-the-murdered-jews-of-europe/), then shopped for souvenirs on Friedrichstraße, including Berlin and Appelman ornaments. We stopped at a café before dinner; the coffee guy was from Michigan.

Dinner at [Reinstoff](https://www.visitberlin.de/en/reinstoff) meant another restaurant hidden down an alley. Ksenia was bothered by that, though it was not nearly as murdery as the alley to Cookies Cream. I had a Japanese gin with cherry blossom, a little sappy straight but delightful with Fever-Tree.
