We walked around Rothenburg in the morning on our way to the [Medieval Crime Museum](https://www.rothenburg.de/en/entdecken/top-10/kriminalmuseum), with its history of crime and punishment, then took the train to Munich and checked into [Hotel Torbräu](https://www.torbraeu.de/).

We spent the last of our cash on [Neuschwanstein](https://www.neuschwanstein.de/englisch/palace/index.htm) tickets and immediately had to find more money. Ksenia panicked; our great Munich adventure began with trying not to get robbed in a seedy part of town, rather than sightseeing. Dinner at the [Hofbräuhaus](https://www.hofbraeuhaus.de/en/) helped: the sausages were really good, though the service could have been better. On the way to the currency exchange, Ksenia got distracted by the Rathaus. “So pretty!”
