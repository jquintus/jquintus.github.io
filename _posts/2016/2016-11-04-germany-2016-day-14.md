We took a day trip to the [Dachau Memorial Site](https://www.kz-gedenkstaette-dachau.de/en/). We saw a gas chamber that may or may not have been used, a crematorium that was used very often, and a lot of reconstructed space. Parts of the site had been used for leatherworks, factories, and restaurants after the war.

Lunch was at McDonald's. Back in Munich, we walked through the [English Garden](https://www.munich.travel/en/pois/sports-leisure/english-garden), stopped at a Japanese tea house, and watched the standing wave with surfers.

Dinner at Weiss Haus—probably [Weisses Bräuhaus](https://www.weisses-brauhaus-tal.de/)—brought tasty weissbier and IPA. The suckling pig was not as good as the pork belly. We played cards at the hotel bar afterward.
