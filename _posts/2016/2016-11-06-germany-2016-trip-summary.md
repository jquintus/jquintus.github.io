We started in Berlin, followed the Rhine to Cologne, caught up with friends near Stuttgart and Ulm, spent a rainy day in Rothenburg, and finished in Munich. We covered a lot of German history, a lot of Christmas shops, several pork knuckles, and one restaurant alley that looked like a Batman crime scene.

## Where We Stayed

In Munich, [Hotel Torbräu](https://www.torbraeu.de/) was our base for the castles and city walks. We also stayed in hotels in Berlin, Cologne, the Stuttgart area, Ulm, and Rothenburg.

## Places and Tours

Berlin gave us [Berlin Cathedral](https://www.berlinerdom.de/en/visiting/), the [DDR Museum](https://www.ddr-museum.de/en), the [East Side Gallery](https://www.visitberlin.de/en/east-side-gallery), and a long walking tour through its Nazi and Cold War history. The former Stasi prison at [Hohenschönhausen](https://www.stiftung-hsh.de/en/) was terrifying. In Cologne, we toured the [cathedral](https://www.koelner-dom.de/en/), made chocolate at the [Chocolate Museum](https://www.schokoladenmuseum.de/en/), and found Max Liebermann at the [Wallraf–Richartz Museum](https://www.wallraf.museum/).

The [Ludwigsburg pumpkin festival](https://www.blueba.de/en/) and Rothenburg's [Night Watchman's Tour](https://www.nightwatchman.de/index.php?active=Home&lang=en) were much lighter days. We also visited the [Medieval Crime Museum](https://www.rothenburg.de/en/entdecken/top-10/kriminalmuseum), [Linderhof Palace](https://www.schloss.bayern.de/englisch/palace/uebers/linderhof.htm), [Neuschwanstein](https://www.neuschwanstein.de/englisch/palace/index.htm), the two [Pinakothek](https://www.pinakothek-der-moderne.de/en/) [museums](https://www.pinakothek.de/en/visit/neue-pinakothek), and the [Dachau Memorial Site](https://www.kz-gedenkstaette-dachau.de/en/). Munich ended with [Marienplatz](https://www.munich.travel/en/pois/urban-districts/marienplatz), the [English Garden](https://www.munich.travel/en/pois/sports-leisure/english-garden), and a lazy last walk to the [Siegestor](https://www.muenchen.de/en/sights/siegestor).

## Vacation Quips
