Pretzels and butter from the grocery store were breakfast before a long walking tour of Berlin. Our guide, Sami Noble, gave us a 2½-hour overview and then a four-hour Third Reich tour. We had currywurst and a Berliner along the way.

We covered the [Brandenburg Gate](https://www.berlin.de/en/attractions-and-sights/3560266-3104052-brandenburg-gate.en.html), the [Memorial to the Murdered Jews of Europe](https://www.stiftung-denkmal.de/en/memorials/the-memorial-to-the-murdered-jews-of-europe/), the former bunker site, the [Topography of Terror](https://www.topographie.de/en/), [Checkpoint Charlie](https://www.visitberlin.de/en/checkpoint-charlie), the [Reichstag](https://www.bundestag.de/en/visittheBundestag), and pieces of the Berlin Wall. The tour continued through Bebelplatz, Gendarmenmarkt, Tiergarten, and the Jewish Quarter. Germany has done a good job being honest about and accepting its history.

At [Brauhaus Lemke](https://www.lemke.berlin/) we had pilsner, pork knuckle, and bread with lard. Ksenia loved the bread and lard.
