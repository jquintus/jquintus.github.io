At the [Chocolate Museum](https://www.schokoladenmuseum.de/en/), we watched chocolate being made and then bought some. An amazing hot chocolate and raspberry cake made lunch. We went to the [Wallraf–Richartz Museum](https://www.wallraf.museum/) after that. Max Liebermann's line stuck with me: “A well-painted beet is every bit as good as a well-painted Madonna.” I came away liking Cross, an Impressionist.

We wandered past the Rathaus, a gay wedding, and people dressed as Berliners and pandas. At [Brauhaus zum Prinzen](https://zum-prinzen.com/) I learned the local beer routine: roughly fifteen seconds after putting down an empty glass, a refill appeared. There were chestnuts in the street, and we ate at [Früh](https://www.frueh-am-dom.de/) again. The game stew was excellent. We stopped at a bar for another Kölsch, the last of the big three.
