We got to Rothenburg by way of four trains. At lunch in the main square, Ksenia was so overwhelmed by how pretty everything was that making a decision became difficult. We both had venison stew.

We spent hours wandering around town: a garden by the western wall, a walk along the wall, the [Christmas Museum](https://www.kaethe-wohlfahrt.com/de/museum), and a lot of Christmas shopping. We also did the [Night Watchman's Tour](https://www.nightwatchman.de/index.php?active=Home&lang=en). By the end, my poor feet were soaked.
