Breakfast at Harry's came with a nice woman who helped us with the menu. We bought insoles for my feet, wandered the market, and went into [Ulm Minster](https://www.ulmer-muenster.de/en). Alex and Bine surprised us there. “It's been a while” was an understatement.

We wandered through old Ulm, which was very pretty. We saw [Schiefes Haus](https://tourismus.ulm.de/de/entdecken/sehen-und-erleben/sehenswuerdigkeiten/historisches/schiefes-haus-ulm), the hotel leaning over the water, and the leaning [Butcher's Tower](https://tourismus.ulm.de/en/discover/ulm-and-neu-ulm/sights/historical-sights/metzgerturm-ulm). Then we drove out to see friends for tea and cake. I saw Willi and Gertha, and we saw Alex's house being built. Dinner together was very good, and all that catching up made for a lovely day.
