Pretzels for breakfast, then a trip to the post office to mail postcards. We walked to the [DDR Museum](https://www.ddr-museum.de/en), where my conclusions were that communism sucked and the wallpaper was hideous. We went through Nikolaiviertel, the rebuilt old center of Berlin, and had lunch at a potato restaurant: potato soup with pork strips, plus sausage with potatoes and mixed vegetables.

We walked to the [East Side Gallery](https://www.visitberlin.de/en/east-side-gallery), a stretch of the remaining Berlin Wall, and stopped at [St. Nicholas Church](https://www.stadtmuseum.de/en/museum/st-nicholas-church) and [Café Spreeblick](https://www.spreeblick-nikolaiviertel.de/) for dessert and tea. Back on Friedrichstraße, I looked at Birkenstocks that cost €65 for one pair; we picked up Christmas CDs at [Dussmann](https://www.kulturkaufhaus.de/de/kulturkaufhaus) and snacks before calling it a day.
