We climbed into the crypts at [St. Stephen's Cathedral](https://www.stephanskirche.at/). Some of the stained glass had survived; the rest was less pretty than Cologne's cathedral. At [Mozarthaus](https://www.mozarthausvienna.at/en/), three floors covered Mozart's life, history, and music, including one floor of his actual apartment.

A light lunch at [Café Hawelka](https://hawelka.at/en/) brought espresso that was okay but still coffee, a very good radler, and apple strudel that was warm, tangy, and maybe the best yet. Ksenia had iced coffee with vanilla ice cream, whipped cream, and chocolate powder. When I asked for a menu, the waiter said, “I am the menu!”

At [Meierei im Stadtpark](https://www.austria.info/en-us/locations/restaurant-steirereck/), we had beef tartare and roasted short ribs. The ribs were drier than I expected; the sour cherry tart was good. At [Loos American Bar](https://www.loosbar.at/en), I had an English Blackthorn and a De Rigueur; Ksenia had a Pussy Foot.
