We started a [Vienna walking tour](https://www.wien.info/en/walks-in-vienna-131292) at Albertinaplatz with Peter. The route went through the Albertina, Sacher Hotel, the memorial for fascism, Hofburg Garden, Neue Hofburg Palace and the National Library, Maria Theresa, Mozart, Heldenplatz, St. Stephen's Cathedral, and plenty more.

Lunch at [Weibel's Wirtshaus](https://weibel.at/) was Wiener schnitzel—not bad—and a local beer with a good smell. We also visited the National Library. At the [Albertina](https://www.albertina.at/en/) we saw Monet, Picasso, and Cézanne, among others: Monet's water lilies, Venice's pink clouds, and a watercolor landscape that stuck with me. Dinner at [Zu den 3 Hacken](https://www.3hacken.com/) was bratwurst and apple strudel.
