We walked past a bust of Franz Joseph I on the way to the [Schönbrunn Palace](https://www.schoenbrunn.at/en/). Next came the open-air opera and food market, with a snack from a stand, then Karlskirche and its statue of a begging king.

At the [Belvedere](https://www.belvedere.at/en), we saw van Gogh's *The Plains of Auvers*, Klimt's *The Kiss*, Rodin's *Eve*, Monet's *Path in Monet's Garden* and *The Chef*, Manet's *Dame in a Fur*, Renoir's *After the Bath*, and Hubert Bayer's *Foliage*, *Mountain Lake*, and *Diving Bird*.

Back at [Gigerl](https://www.gigerl.at/) for dinner: Wiener schnitzel for me and Kasnspätzle for Ksenia. Then a Mozart concert at Karlskirche. Ksenia says I fell asleep. I disagree.
