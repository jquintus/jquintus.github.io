Our food tour covered salami and sausages—including deer, horse, and pig—plus pickles, goulash, cheese curds, wine, and lemonade with cardamom and ginger. The goulash was *so tasty*. The cheese was fried and served with sour cream; the wine tasting came with generous pours.

Dinner at [Gettó Gulyás](https://gettogulyas.hu/en/homepage/) brought bone marrow, a mojito that was too hot, and rooster testicles. Then we went to [Strudel House](https://reteshaz.com/en) for cherry strudel.
