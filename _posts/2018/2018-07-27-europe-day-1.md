Ksenia picked me up from the airport with hugs. We wandered Budapest and had dinner at [Két Szerecsen](https://www.ketszerecsen.hu/?lang=e): a Budapest platter with salami and foie gras, and a Magyar Ham with Hungarian ratatouille.

Afterward we walked down Andrássy út, Budapest's Champs-Élysées, and saw a bunch of Ferraris revving their engines in [Heroes' Square](https://www.budapestinfo.hu/en/place-des-heros). We made it to [Vajdahunyad Castle](https://ligetbudapest.hu/en/vajdahunyad-castle) and the statue of Anonymous.
