We walked to Deák tér and dipped our toes in the pool above the aquarium nightclub, then caught a bus to [Memento Park](https://www.mementopark.hu/en/). Lunch was at [Bigfish](https://thebigfish.hu/en/), followed by the [House of Terror](https://terrorhaza.hu/en).

Dinner at [Cyrano](https://cyrano.hu/) started with goose liver, figs, strawberry jam, and shortbread. There was a strawberry and ginger yogurt amuse-bouche, duck leg with foie gras, sour cherry pálinka, and Williams pear pálinka. We ended the night with a boat ride on the Danube.
