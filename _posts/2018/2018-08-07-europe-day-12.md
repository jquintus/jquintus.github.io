At the [Mucha Museum](https://www.mucha.cz/en/), I came away with two facts: it is small and it has a lot of good stuff.

Lunch at [Kantýna](https://www.kantyna.ambi.cz/) worked on the same kind of order-ticket system as Hill Country in New York. Ksenia had potato pancakes and kielbasa. I had a bone-in pork chop, pulled beef, prime rib, and pork belly. Great meat options.

We went on to the Franciscan Garden and Wenceslas Square. At the [Museum of Communism](https://muzeumkomunismu.cz/en/), we saw exhibits about Czechoslovak history. Dinner at [King Solomon](https://prague.eu/en/objevujte/5th-district-restaurant-cafe-by-king-solomon/) was goose drumstick for me and stuffed cabbage for Ksenia.
