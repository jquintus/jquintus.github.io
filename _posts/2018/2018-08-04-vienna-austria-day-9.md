Ksenia's much-anticipated visit to the [Spanish Riding School](https://www.srs.at/en/) came first. The regular show with the studs was off-season, so the mares were in town instead. Ksenia had hearts in her eyes the whole time. I nodded off a few times.

At [Demel](https://www.demel.com/), the coffee was better. Ksenia had a cake that was essentially an oversized macaron; I had strawberry cheesecake. We split a mini Sacher cake and I had a melange. We went back to the Riding School for a guided tour and a walk through the stables.

Outside the Rathaus, a fair had food vendors and a screen showing movies. We split a bärg spitzer and a bratwurst. Dinner at [Gigerl](https://www.gigerl.at/) for the third time, then a concert of Mozart's *Requiem* at Karlskirche. Ksenia says I fell asleep. I disagree.
