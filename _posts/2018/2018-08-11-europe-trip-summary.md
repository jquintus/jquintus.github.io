## Where we stayed

In Vienna we stayed at the [Hotel Royal](https://www.hotelroyal.at/en/), right in Old Town. In Prague, we checked in to [the King's Court Hotel](https://www.hotelkingscourt.cz/). We also stayed in Budapest and Ludwigsburg.

## Places and detours

Budapest gave us [Memento Park](https://www.mementopark.hu/en/), the [House of Terror](https://terrorhaza.hu/en), the [Hungarian National Gallery](https://muzej.hu/en/museum/hungarian-national-gallery), and a playful pair of polar bears at the [Budapest Zoo](https://zoobudapest.com/en/home/). A boat ride on the Danube finished one day.

Vienna packed in the [Spanish Riding School](https://www.srs.at/en/), [Schönbrunn](https://www.schoenbrunn.at/en/), the [Belvedere](https://www.belvedere.at/en), the [Albertina](https://www.albertina.at/en/), [St. Stephen's](https://www.stephanskirche.at/), and concerts at Karlskirche. Ksenia says I fell asleep at the Mozart concert. I disagree.

In Prague we toured [the castle](https://www.hrad.cz/en/prague-castle-for-visitors), saw the [Mucha Museum](https://www.mucha.cz/en/) and the [Museum of Communism](https://muzeumkomunismu.cz/en/), walked the Jewish Quarter, and fought station closures and ticket machines before finally reaching Old Town Square. We finished with family, a wine festival, and lakes around Ludwigsburg.
