## The Trip

**July 22–August 5, 2017. Fifteen days, four cities:** Kraków, Gdańsk, Warsaw, and Wrocław, with side trips to Auschwitz-Birkenau, Wieliczka, and Książ Castle. Ksenia greeted me at the airport with flowers and hugs. We ended with too much bread and one last walk around Wrocław.

## Where We Ate and Drank

### Kraków

| Place | Rating | What I thought |
| --- | --- | --- |
| King's Restaurant | — | Beef tartare, foie gras, duck, wild boar, and mushroom ice cream on our first night. |
| [Pod Nosem][pod-nosem] | — | Beef tartare and duck with foie gras for me; more foie gras and strawberries for Ksenia. |
| [Jama Michalika][jama] | — | Tea at the old home of the Green Balloon cabaret. |
| [The Black Duck][black-duck] | — | Salmon tartare and Old Polish duck with red cabbage. |
| [Szara Gęś (the Grey Goose)][grey-goose] | — | Foie gras ice cream and roast goose before our flight. |

We also had pierogi, duck pierogi, Polish crêpes, and blueberries between the named stops.

### Gdańsk

| Place | Rating | What I thought |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Gvara][gvara] | ★★★☆☆ 3/5 | The goose liver with apple sauce wasn't for me, but the ham hocks were tasty and tender. Ksenia liked her cold borscht and potato pancakes. |
| [Pod Łososiem][salmon] | ★★☆☆☆ 2/5 | Dinner was okay and the place seemed past its prime. The cherry flambé dessert was good. |
| [Craft Cocktails][craft] | — | Slow Fader and a gin cocktail; the latter took too long and got warm. |
| NaLeśnikowa | — | Local crêpes for breakfast. |
| [Lodziarnia Miś][mis] | — | “Famous” lemon ice cream, though the flavor I wanted had sold out. |
| [Bar Turystyczny][bar] | ★★★★★ 5/5 | Simple, well-executed pork cutlets. Dinner for both of us cost 32 złoty. |

We also stopped for a John Collins, snacks, and tea after watching the candy makers at Ciuciu.

### Warsaw

| Place | Rating | What I thought |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Czerwony Wieprz][hog] | — | I ate from the “Dignitaries” menu; Ksenia ate from the “Proletariat” menu. |
| [Croque Madame][croque] | — | Better-than-average lemonade. |
| [Być Może][byc-moze] | — | Fried eggs, bacon, and pancakes for me; scrambled eggs for Ksenia. |

There was also a zapiekanka, pierogi, ice cream, and dinner with friends.

### Wrocław

| Place | Rating | What I thought |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Dwór Polski][dwor] | ★★☆☆☆ 2/5 | Roast stag and duck with cherries. So-so. |
| [Graciarnia][graciarnia] | ★★★★☆ 4/5 | Sobótka was clean, crisp, a little bitter, and an excellent summer beer. |
| [Café Vincent][vincent] | — | Breakfast came with so much bread that we ordered too much. |

I also had an open-faced roast-beef-and-fried-egg sandwich for breakfast before touring the university.

## Where We Stayed

- [Hotel Jan][jan] on Grodzka Street in Kraków.
- [Mamaison Hotel Le Regina][mamaison] in Warsaw—fancier than I expected.

We also stayed in Gdańsk and Wrocław.

## Places and Days Worth Remembering

The [Auschwitz-Birkenau Memorial][auschwitz] was a difficult, necessary day. Kraków also gave us [Wawel][wawel], the [Wieliczka Salt Mine][salt], St. Mary's Basilica, and a Jewish Quarter tour. In Gdańsk we visited the [European Solidarity Centre][ecs] and the [Museum of the Second World War][ww2]. Warsaw gave us the [Warsaw Rising Museum][rising], the Rising anniversary ceremony at the cemetery, and Ksenia's verdict from the top of the [Palace of Culture and Science][palace]: the best view in town, because the building isn't in it. In Wrocław, the [Racławice Panorama][panorama], [Centrum Historii Zajezdnia][depot], dwarf statues, the [Pan Tadeusz Museum][tadeusz], and [Książ Castle][castle] filled out the trip. The train to Wrocław provided five hours of its own entertainment, without air conditioning.
