I went back to the [Sagrada Família](https://sagradafamilia.org/en/home) in the morning. The sun wasn't hitting the blue windows the way I had hoped, but I still wanted another look before leaving. At breakfast in the hotel, we ate the last of the ham we'd bought.

We wandered through Gràcia, shopped a little, and picked up sweets at [Pastisseria Príncep](https://tartapan.com/pasteleria-principe-barcelona/). Lunch at [Bar Mut](https://www.barmut.com/) included cava, steak with foie, mussels, and potatoes with egg yolk and shrimp.

After a surprise siesta, we walked to [Bobby Gin](https://www.bobbygin.com/en/) where I had a serious round of gin and tonics. The bartender explained that here vermouth is for sipping, not mixing, unless you are making a Negroni. Dinner was at [Tapeo del Born](https://www.tapeoborn.cat/): a foie burger, foie and egg with mushrooms, Iberian ribs, sweetbreads, and Catalan cream, which was good. It felt a little more touristy than some of our other meals, but the food was still good. Ksenia liked it more than I did. We went back to pack, shower, and face a very early flight.

Part of my March 2017 Barcelona trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2017/2017-03-12-barcelona-2017-trip-summary %}).
