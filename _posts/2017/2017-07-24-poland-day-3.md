We took a walking tour of Kraków: the main square, the Cloth Hall, the Barbican, St. Mary's and St. Barbara's churches, the old town hall tower, Collegium Maius, and [Wawel][wawel]. The tower is apparently leaning about 50 centimeters off vertical. The square is huge; our guide traced it to the meeting of two trading routes.

I found duck pierogi; Ksenia found blueberries. Then we went underground at the [Wieliczka Salt Mine][salt].

Dinner at [Pod Nosem][pod-nosem]—“under the nose”—was beef tartare and duck with foie gras for me. Ksenia had foie gras with strawberries and filet mignon with foie gras, plus strawberries in yogurt mousse.
