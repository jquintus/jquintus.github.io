We went to the [European Solidarity Centre][ecs] and learned about Lech Wałęsa and the Solidarity movement. I wrote down that Solidarity candidates won 260 of 261 available seats when voting opened up around 1990.

Dinner at [Pod Łososiem][salmon] was okay, though it seemed past its prime. The old-fashioned place was fancy; vanilla ice cream with cherry flambé was good. We followed it with drinks at [Craft Cocktails][craft]: a Slow Fader with rum, Campari, elderflower, and bitters, and a gin cocktail with Beefeater, lemon, and an egg-white foam. The second drink took too long and got warm.
