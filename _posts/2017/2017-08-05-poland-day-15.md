Breakfast at [Café Vincent][vincent] came with bread on everything. We ordered too much bread. Then we climbed to the top of St. Mary Magdalene's Basilica: many steps, and the same stairs for going up and down, but a great view.

The [Pan Tadeusz Museum][tadeusz] had interesting rooms about the book and its author and an audio guide worth getting. One display had a seal from a noble family in the story. Lunch back at [Karczma Lwowska][lwowska] was marinated pears in vinegar—odd, but surprisingly good—and kielbasa and potatoes. We walked past one of the prisons where Chris had been held.
