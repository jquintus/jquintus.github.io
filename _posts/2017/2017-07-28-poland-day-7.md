We walked Długa past the Golden Gate, Neptune's Fountain, and Green Gate, then along the Motława. Breakfast was local crêpes at NaLeśnikowa. We made our way to the [Museum of the Second World War][ww2] and had “famous” lemon ice cream at [Lodziarnia Miś][mis]. Ksenia got English cream; I think mine was sold out.

The weather was bright and sunny. Ksenia asked whether I wanted to buy amber. “I? No.” We bought amber. We also went into St. Mary's Church: very white, with barely any stained glass.

We watched buskers breakdance to too much pop, watched candy being made by hand at Ciuciu, and stopped for a John Collins, snacks, and tea. Dinner at [Bar Turystyczny][bar] cost 32 złoty for both of us. Simple food, well executed, like pork cutlets should be. The day's meals came to around 100 złoty—less than $30 for the two of us.
