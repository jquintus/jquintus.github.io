Breakfast was an open-faced roast-beef sandwich with a fried egg. At the university we found the spot where Chris and Alicja met, a courtyard that Ksenia thought would make good nightmare material, and the place where the largest student protests happened.

The [Racławice Panorama][panorama] is a giant three-dimensional painting of Kościuszko's battle against the Russians. Poland won the battle, though not the war or its independence. At [Centrum Historii Zajezdnia][depot] we followed Wrocław's history from World War II through Solidarity.

Dinner at [Dwór Polski][dwor] was so-so: roast stag for me and roast duck with cherries for Ksenia. We met Alicja and Chris at [Graciarnia][graciarnia] for drinks. My Sobótka beer was really good: clean, crisp, a little bitter, an excellent summer beer without being light. Ksenia wanted an apple cider, but it was out of stock. We met more of their Polish friends later.
