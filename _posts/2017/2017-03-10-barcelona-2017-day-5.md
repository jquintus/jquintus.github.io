We walked down La Rambla, keeping an eye on our wallets and phones, and stopped at [La Boqueria](https://www.boqueria.barcelona/home): a sandwich, a cone of meat, and fresh juice. Then we wandered through the Gothic Quarter, past the cathedral, into shops, and into a paper store with hand-bound leather notebooks. That store, [Papirum](https://www.barnacentre.com/es/punto-interes/papirvm), was very much my kind of place.

Lunch was at El Nou Pi Antic: Estrella beer, ham toast, lamb ribs, and crema catalana. At [Palau Güell](https://www.palauguell.cat/en) I liked the early Gaudí details, though the curvy, sloping roof was not great for my fear of heights. We also visited [MACBA](https://www.macba.cat/en/), which was emphatically not our cup of tea.

Part of my March 2017 Barcelona trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2017/2017-03-12-barcelona-2017-trip-summary %}).
