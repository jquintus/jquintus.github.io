We arrived in Warsaw and checked into [Mamaison Hotel Le Regina][hotel], fancier than I expected. After lunch we wandered “Old Town”—more like New Town, if you ask me. We passed the Royal Castle, the Presidential Palace, and the park that used to be an airport, the first in Poland. I ate a zapiekanka and we had pierogi for dinner.
