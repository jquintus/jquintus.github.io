Barcelona gave us plenty of reasons to keep eating: Iberian ham, foie gras, croquettes, tomato bread, and gin and tonics. The [Devour Barcelona food tour](https://devourtours.com/destinations/spain/barcelona/) and our two meals at [La Pubilla](https://www.lapubilladeltaulat.es/en/) were my food highlights. We spent the rest of the trip trying to fit Gaudí's buildings, museums, and still more meals between them.

## Where we ate and drank

The [Devour Barcelona food tour](https://devourtours.com/destinations/spain/barcelona/) was ★★★★★. We stopped for sausage on tomato bread and cava at [Can Tosca](https://www.graciacomerc.com/restaurantes-bares-pubs-gracia/can-tosca); olives at Conserves Glòria; ham at Fàbregas; cheese and jams at La Trobada del Gourmet; olive oil at [Oli Sal](https://olisal.com/en/content/4-about-us); tomato bread and croquettes at [L'Anxoveta](https://es.restaurantguru.com/LAnxoveta-Barcelona); pastries at [Pastisseria Príncep](https://tartapan.com/pasteleria-principe-barcelona/); vermouth, pickled anchovies, and sausage at [Bodega Cal Pep](https://www.bodegacalpepgracia.com/ca/); prepared food at La Botigueta del Bon Menjar; and *cremat* at [Pastisseria Ideal](https://rutadelsemblematics.cat/barcelona-pastisseria-ideal.html). The small group made the tour even better.

We also had an enormous dinner at a place I didn't name. The waiter kept thinking we were done ordering; we weren't. One lunch near the Sagrada Família was a miss: slow service and microwaved food that was still cold.

## Where we stayed

[Hotel Praktik Bakery](https://www.hotelpraktikbakery.com/en/) put us above a bakery with some of the best bread and pastries we found in the city. The location and rain showers were great too.

## What we saw

The [Sagrada Família](https://sagradafamilia.org/en/home) was my favorite part of Barcelona that I couldn't eat or drink. The stained-glass light changed every time I looked. [Casa Batlló](https://www.casabatllo.es/en/) felt like an underwater fairy tale, and [Park Güell](https://parkguell.barcelona/en) was gorgeous even with the crowds. We also visited [Palau Güell](https://www.palauguell.cat/en), the [Picasso Museum](https://museupicassobcn.cat/en/), and [Sant Pau](https://santpaubarcelona.org/en/). [MACBA](https://www.macba.cat/en/) wasn't for us.
