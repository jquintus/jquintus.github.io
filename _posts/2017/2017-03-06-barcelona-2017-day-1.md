We landed in Barcelona around noon and started with a croissant and fig tart from the bakery at [Hotel Praktik Bakery](https://www.hotelpraktikbakery.com/en/), where we were staying. That was a promising beginning.

At [Cervecería Catalana](https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/agendajove/en/detall/cerveseria-catalana_75990341849.html) we ordered Iberian ham and loin, fried cuttlefish, mushrooms and asparagus with garlic, and a filet with foie gras. The filet and foie were great. We wandered through orange trees under a blue sky before visiting [Casa Batlló](https://www.casabatllo.es/en/). Gaudí made the inside feel like an underwater fairy tale.

Dinner at [La Pepita](https://lapepita.squarespace.com/la-pepita-en) meant more Iberian ham, grilled artichoke with celery mashed potatoes, shrimp, patatas bravas, a salmon sandwich with foie gras, and more gin and tonic. This city is off to an excellent start.

Part of my March 2017 Barcelona trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2017/2017-03-12-barcelona-2017-trip-summary %}).
