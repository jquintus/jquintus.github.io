The [Warsaw Rising Museum][rising] had so much information and so many hands-on exhibits that I wished the playing and the context had been more clearly separated. I finished the Sunday *New York Times* crossword in 2 hours 10 minutes. Lots of clues to check.

At Powązki Cemetery, Ksenia got lost twice. We stopped in a church with nice stained glass. Dinner was at [Czerwony Wieprz][hog], a Soviet/Russian-themed restaurant with menus for “the Dignitaries” and “the Proletariat.” I had a Bruce Willis drink, beef stroganoff, and Tito's pork. Ksenia had lard with bread, borscht, and dumplings. I ate like a dignitary; Ksenia ate like a worker.
