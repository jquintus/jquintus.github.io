## Scotch

| Whisky at [Fiddler's Arms][fiddlers-arms] | What I thought |
| --- | --- |
| [Glenfiddich 12][glenfiddich-12] | Speyside. Light caramel, a honey/caramel color, and a very light peaty finish. |
| [Glen Scotia Double Cask][glen-scotia] | Campbeltown. Oaky nose; I wasn't picking much up before the finish. |
| [Caol Ila 12][caol-ila] | Islay. Peat and smoke on the nose, a smooth start, and strong smoke at the finish. |

We visited [Surgeons' Hall][surgeons-hall] for centuries of surgical tools and preparations, including the Hall of Pathology.

[Thomas J. Walls][thomas-walls] was packed. We had tea and sandwiches, a scone with clotted cream, and more tea.

At [Greyfriars Kirkyard][greyfriars], we saw Tom Riddle's grave and remembered the story of Greyfriars Bobby. The dog stood by his master's grave for twelve years, then got buried outside the cemetery because he was a dog. And they patted themselves on the back like they weren't all arses.

We walked around Old Town and stopped at Fiddler's Arms for Scotch before dinner at [Mussel Inn][mussel-inn]. We'd been here with D on our first trip. Ksenia had a half pot of mussels in white wine. I had a smoked salmon appetizer and a half pot of the applewood-smoked special.

Then Werewolf: The Gameshow. The title was a lie. Two werewolves, plural, and no gameshow. Ksenia liked it more than I did.

Part of our August 2025 Scotland trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2025/2025-08-31-scotland-trip-summary %}).
