## Scotch Tallies

| Scotches drunk today | Unique Scotches today |

Breakfast at [The Bowmore House][bowmore-house] came with a house-blend dram. A useful start to a day of distillery visits.

### Bunnahabhain

We started at [Bunnahabhain][bunnahabhain] around ten. The 16-year-old Ruby Port Finish was spicy, fruity, and sweet up front. The nose reminded me of paint.

The 2014 Canasta was something else: caramel smell, caramel color, caramel taste. Chocolate, too. Like a candy store.

[Toiteach A Dhà][bunnahabhain-toiteach] was lightly peated, light in color, and easy to drink.

### Bruichladdich

At [Bruichladdich][bruichladdich], we tasted straight from the casks. They make unpeated Bruichladdich, heavily peated Port Charlotte, and very heavily peated Octomore. Also [The Botanist gin][botanist-gin].

| Whisky | What I thought |
| --- | --- |
| 2009 Bruichladdich, 16 years old | Berries on the finish, iodine, and a malty flavor from the Bere barley. No peat. |
| 2015 Port Charlotte | A sweet nose that carried into the first taste, with smoke on the finish. “That's nice.” |
| 2011 Octomore | Super caramel, an earthy nose, and plenty of peat. The smoke was all in the finish. |
| [Octomore Polyphonic][octomore-polyphonic] | Funky, almost mushroomy on the nose. Smoky but smooth. Nice! |

### Kilchoman

[Kilchoman][kilchoman] was a great tour. Lunch was Cullen skink and a selection of sandwiches, like high tea. Christie led the tasting.

We literally watched the guys shovel barley while drinking the 100% Islay release. Hard to improve on that timing.

| Whisky | What I thought |
| --- | --- |
| [100% Islay, 14th Edition][kilchoman-14] | Oily, with bourbon and sherry casks. An “ideal breakfast dram.” |
| [Machir Bay][kilchoman-machir] | Sweet on the nose, floral, and citrusy. |
| Loch Gorm | Deep color, Oloroso sherry casks. |
| Distillery exclusive, 13-year-old ex-Buffalo Trace cask | Light in color, with some of the mash on the nose. |

There was a bonus taste at the end, too.

### More Whisky, Then Oysters

The [Islay Whisky Vault][whisky-vault] sold old, rare, and unusual whisky. Somebody's description of the job: “I'm selling art.”

There was [Ardnahoe Infinite Loch][ardnahoe-infinite], fruity and menthol on the nose; [Bowmore 15][bowmore-15], with a little smoke and a floral finish; [Lagavulin 8][lagavulin-8], salty and seaweedy; and a Caol Ila distillery release with salt, smoke, sweetness, and an oily texture. An [Ardnahoe Inaugural Release][ardnahoe-inaugural] and more Bruichladdich rounded out the day: a special-cask 16-year-old and a dark-caramel 14-year-old with rich, warm spices.

Dinner back at the [Lochside][lochside]: four oysters and the scallop special.
