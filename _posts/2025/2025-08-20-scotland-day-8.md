## Scotch

| Whisky | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Loch Lomond 10 (expression unrecorded) | With dinner at [Element][element]. |

We spent time watching street performers on the [Royal Mile][royal-mile]: a flute looper, a fire juggler, a knife juggler, and a magician doing cups and balls.

Lunch was at [Filling Station][filling-station]: a chicken burger, fish and chips, and strawberry lemonade.

Knightclub was like *Fight Club* with monks and knights. Think *Fight Club* meets *A Knight's Tale* on *Monty Python and the Holy Grail*'s budget. Very silly.

We also saw How to Become a Movie Star. We just grabbed tickets at the box office. It was OK.

Back outside, there were more street performers, including a blues guitarist and a basketball man.

Dinner was at Element. I had Chicken Balmoral, and we also had mushroom stroganoff and a Scotch egg. The drinks were Loch Lomond 10 and [Inch's cider][inchs].

Part of our August 2025 Scotland trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2025/2025-08-31-scotland-trip-summary %}).
