Lunch was at [Mangio](https://mangio.co.uk/) near St. Paul's. We found this little fresh Italian place last time we were here. I have no idea what I ordered, but it was good. Ksenia had focaccia and a tart.

At [Tate Britain](https://www.tate.org.uk/visit/tate-britain), we saw the [Lee Miller exhibition](https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-britain/lee-miller). It followed her from Vogue model and photographer to avant-garde artist and war correspondent. Her work at Dachau and in Hitler's apartment was especially powerful.

Then: afternoon tea at [Brown's Hotel](https://www.roccofortehotels.com/hotels-and-resorts/brown-s-hotel/dining/the-drawing-room/). Ksenia chose Earl Grey, and I had chai. There were egg salad, prawn, chicken salad, smoked salmon, and cucumber-goat cheese sandwiches; plain and almond scones; apple cheesecake, chocolate cake, carrot cake, and something with gingerbread. I can't remember the other two desserts, but I certainly remember that there were desserts.

We ended the day with *Paddington The Musical* at the Savoy Theatre. Ksenia cried a lot. 🐻

Part of our November 2025 London trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2025/2025-11-13-london-nov-2025-trip-summary %}).
