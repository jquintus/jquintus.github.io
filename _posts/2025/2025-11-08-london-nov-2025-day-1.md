We landed at Heathrow, where our original plane was broken. After moving to a new gate and waiting for a replacement plane, we were told to expect a two-hour delay. We eventually made it into London, but our room at [Hotel Indigo London Kensington](https://www.ihg.com/hotelindigo/hotels/gb/en/london/lonlk/hoteldetail) wasn't ready. We dropped our bags and went to the [Victoria and Albert Museum](https://www.vam.ac.uk/south-kensington/visit).

Lunch was at the V&A café's deli counter. I managed an hour-long nap at the hotel afterward, then we returned to explore the museum. It was a maze. We eventually found the furniture and photography exhibits—something for everyone.

Dinner at [Kitchen W8](https://www.kitchenw8.com/) was a long parade of dishes, including yellow chanterelles, deer carpaccio, partridge, pear and sage, smoky truffle mashed potatoes for the table, and clementine sorbet.

Part of our November 2025 London trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2025/2025-11-13-london-nov-2025-trip-summary %}).
