## Scotland 2025

We landed in Edinburgh and took a cab to [Hotel Indigo on Princes Street][hotel-indigo]. Our first food stop was a waffle in the park by the hotel, followed by a visit to [Wojtek's statue in Princes Street Gardens][wojtek].

Then straight into the [Fringe][fringe]. At Rock & Roll Magician, I got picked to help with a card trick involving a ripped corner. Three stars for that one.

Dinner was at [Duck & Waffle][duck-waffle], by the hotel. We had crispy pig ear, foie gras brûlée, and, naturally, duck and waffle. Ksenia had [Thistly Cross cider][thistly-cross], while I started my Scotch drinking with Tamdhu 15.

We finished the evening with Adult Panto. Our first panto! Also, it was improv. Three stars for that one too.

Part of our August 2025 Scotland trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2025/2025-08-31-scotland-trip-summary %}).
