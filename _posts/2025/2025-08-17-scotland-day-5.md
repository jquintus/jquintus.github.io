We walked up [Calton Hill][calton-hill] for the 360-degree view of Edinburgh, then headed to [New Calton Cemetery][new-calton] to see Robert Stevenson's grave—the grandfather of the writer. From there we went over to [Old Calton Cemetery][old-calton] and its Lincoln statue and American Civil War memorial.

Sunday roast was at [Hawksmoor][hawksmoor]: prime rib with all the fixings. Roast potatoes, Yorkshire pudding, boiled carrots and cabbage, roast garlic, marrow gravy, and sausage stuffing. I also had a [Hepple gin][hepple] and tonic, which reminded me of Bombay Sapphire.

Then back to the [Fringe][fringe]. Gummy Bear War was in Italian with subtitles. Fun, although we think we missed some cultural reference that would have explained the gummy bears' assault on the dinosaurs.

[Anna Hale: Control Freak][anna-hale] was a funny one-person comedic musical about therapy for her anxiety.

Dinner was at [The Botanist][botanist]. Ksenia had the Forager's Feast mushroom burger; I had the chicken kebab. We finished with Married Without Children, a mix of sketches and improv.

Part of our August 2025 Scotland trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2025/2025-08-31-scotland-trip-summary %}).
