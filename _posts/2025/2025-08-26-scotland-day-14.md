Breakfast at [Where The Pancakes Are][pancakes]. Pancakes, naturally.

Then a Shakespeare double feature at [the Globe][globe]: a matinee of *The Merry Wives of Windsor*, followed by *Twelfth Night*.

Between shows, we had our lunch/dinner at [Swan][swan], adjoining the Globe. Bread with Marmite butter, crab, trout for me, and a duck appetizer for Ksenia.
