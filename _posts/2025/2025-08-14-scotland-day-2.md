## Scotch

| Whisky | Where |
| --- | --- |
| [Oban][oban] | With lunch. |
| [Isle of Raasay][raasay] | With dinner at the hotel. |

We started our three-day Skye tour with Billy as our guide. Before we got too far from Edinburgh, he recommended [Dean Village][dean-village]: pretty, with good shops and cafes. Something for our return to the city.

By 9:25 we were at [Loch Lubnaig][loch-lubnaig]. The day's stops also included [Doune Castle][doune-castle]—the Monty Python castle—and [Stirling Bridge][stirling-bridge]. Lunch was at [Lochside Brasserie][lochside-brasserie].

We continued through the Highlands, with the [Five Sisters of Kintail][five-sisters] and [Loch Duich][loch-duich] along the way. We also met Jamie and Margo, a pair of horses.

Dinner was at our hotel: sea bass, butternut squash, and vanilla sorbet with an almond flour brownie and popcorn. I had a Raasay Scotch and [Skye Red beer][skye-red]; Ksenia had [Thistly Cross Scottish Fruits cider][thistly-cross].

Part of our August 2025 Scotland trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2025/2025-08-31-scotland-trip-summary %}).
