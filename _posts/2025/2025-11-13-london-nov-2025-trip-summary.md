## Where we stayed

We stayed at [Hotel Indigo London Kensington](https://www.ihg.com/hotelindigo/hotels/gb/en/london/lonlk/hoteldetail). Its dining room handled both breakfast and our dinner after the Stratford day trip.

## Places and performances

We spent time at the [V&A](https://www.vam.ac.uk/south-kensington/visit), [The Photographers' Gallery](https://thephotographersgallery.org.uk/)—especially its Zofia Rydet and Boris Mikhailov shows—and [Tate Britain's Lee Miller exhibition](https://www.tate.org.uk/whats-on/tate-britain/lee-miller). The [Hunterian Museum](https://hunterianmuseum.org/) was really cool to me and much too creepy for Ksenia.

[Word on the Water](https://www.wordonthewater.co.uk/about-us) was a bookshop on a barge beside the canal. Our rock-and-roll walking tour took us through Denmark Street and Soho, past recording studios, old venues, and the Beatles' rooftop concert site at 3 Savile Row. We also saw [*Paddington The Musical*](https://london.paddingtonthemusical.com/about-the-show/) at the Savoy, and [*Macbeth*](https://www.rsc.org.uk/news/whats-on-this-winter) at the RSC's [Other Place](https://www.rsc.org.uk/your-visit/the-other-place) in Stratford-upon-Avon. Ksenia cried at *Paddington*; I got my rock-history fix.
