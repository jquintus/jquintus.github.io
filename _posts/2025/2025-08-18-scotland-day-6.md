A quick stop at the post office, then another day of the [Fringe][fringe].

First up was [A Parody of the Rings][parody-rings], at Gilded Balloon Patter House. It was a funny, “impromptu” retelling of the three movies. Most of *The Two Towers* and *The Return of the King* got skipped for time.

Then [Earnest?][earnest]. The premise: the actor playing Earnest in *The Importance of Being Earnest* doesn't show up, so the company grabs a “volunteer” from the audience to fill in. Hilarity ensues. Likely the best show we've seen here.

We followed that with A Covert Affair, about two spies in Budapest.


Part of our August 2025 Scotland trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2025/2025-08-31-scotland-trip-summary %}).
