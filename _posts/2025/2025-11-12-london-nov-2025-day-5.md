We took the train to Stratford-upon-Avon. Breakfast at [The Fourteas](https://www.thefourteas.co.uk/) meant a full English, lots of tea, and wartime propaganda in the décor. The little tea room was cozy and yummy, with especially good mushrooms and potato hash browns.

At the [Royal Shakespeare Company's Other Place](https://www.rsc.org.uk/your-visit/the-other-place), we saw [*Macbeth*](https://www.rsc.org.uk/news/whats-on-this-winter) set in a 1970s/80s Glasgow. Sam Heughan—Jamie from *Outlander*—played Macbeth. Then we took the train back to London and had dinner at the hotel.

Part of our November 2025 London trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2025/2025-11-13-london-nov-2025-trip-summary %}).
