Ksenia and I spent August 13–30 in Scotland and London. Three days on Skye, a lot of Edinburgh Fringe shows, four days on Islay, and then London for Shakespeare, Totoro, and Paddington.

Also Scotch. A lot of Scotch.

## The Scotch

[All the Scotch tasting posts]({{ '/tags/scotch-tasting/' | relative_url }}).

### Four Days on Islay

Morag took us from Glasgow to Islay, with stops at Loch Lomond and Inveraray and a ferry ride with great views. We stayed at [The Bowmore House][bowmore-house]. Andrew Jackson was our host, and breakfast came with a house-blend dram.

The distillery days were busy. At [Bruichladdich][bruichladdich], we drank straight from the casks. At [Kilchoman][kilchoman], we watched barley being shoveled while drinking the whisky. [Lagavulin][lagavulin], [Ardbeg][ardbeg], and [Laphroaig][laphroaig] filled most of the next day.

A few that stood out:

| Scotch | What I thought |
| --- | --- |
| [Lagavulin Fèis Ìle 2025, 15-year-old][lagavulin-festival] | One of the best of the trip. Smoke blossomed over warm notes. Moscatel casks. |
| [Octomore 15.3][octomore-153] | The other best of the trip. Oily and super smoky, with dried fruit from the sherry casks. |
| [Kilchoman Sanaig][kilchoman-sanaig] | So good!! |
| Bunnahabhain 2014 Canasta | Caramel smell, color, and taste, plus chocolate. Like a candy store. |
| Port Charlotte 2015 cask | Sweet on the nose and at the start, then smoke on the finish. “That's nice.” |
| [Octomore Polyphonic][octomore-polyphonic] | Funky, almost mushroomy. Smoky but smooth. Nice! |
| [Ardbeg Uigeadail][ardbeg-uigeadail] | Dried fruit and more sherry. |
| [Ardbeg Corryvreckan][ardbeg-corryvreckan] | Peppery, with a tang. |
| Lagavulin 12-year-old Madeira-cask fill-your-own | I tried it to decide whether to buy a bottle. I did not. |

Kilchoman was a great tour, with Cullen skink and a sandwich selection for lunch. Our Ardbeg guide enjoyed slagging us off. At [Kildalton][kildalton], the cross and roofless church were stupid pretty.

We also visited the [Islay Whisky Vault][whisky-vault] for old, rare, and unusual whisky, and finished a long day at the [Bowmore Hotel][bowmore-hotel]. Teresa's verdict on the hotel: a “goddamn delight.”

“Pacing is for losers” made it into the conversation. By the end of August 23, I had crossed out my count and increased it. Twenty-one for the day.

### More Scotch Around Scotland

The [Talisker][talisker] tour on Skye included the [10-year-old][talisker-10], [Wild Blue][talisker-wild-blue], and a distillery exclusive. The 10 had pepper on the finish and sea spray. Wild Blue was salty, with a reddish tint from red-wine casks.

In Inverness I tried a flight of [Caol Ila 12][caol-ila], Macallan 12, and [Balblair 12][balblair]. At a whisky shop in Dunkeld, there were drop-in drams: Glenturret 10, its peated version, Ardnamurchan, and [James Eadie's Trademark X][james-eadie-trademark]. Trademark X was complex but well balanced; the peated Glenturret balanced smoke with chocolate and dark fruit.

Back in Edinburgh, the [Fiddler's Arms][fiddlers-arms] supplied [Glenfiddich 12][glenfiddich-12], [Glen Scotia Double Cask][glen-scotia], and more Caol Ila. The latter started smoothly and finished with strong smoke.

## Places, Tours, and Shows

### Scotland

- Three days touring Skye with Billy: [Loch Lubnaig][loch-lubnaig], [Doune Castle][doune-castle], [Stirling Bridge][stirling-bridge], the [Five Sisters of Kintail][five-sisters], and Loch Duich on the way.
- The [Fairy Glen][fairy-glen], Castle Ewen, Portree, and Talisker on Skye, with sheep, cows, a goat, and waterfalls along the way.
- [Plockton][plockton], [Rogie Falls][rogie-falls], Inverness, and [Leakey's bookshop][leakeys] on the return journey.
- [Dunkeld Cathedral][dunkeld-cathedral], a pretty stone building beside the river, followed by drop-in drams at a whisky shop.
- [Calton Hill][calton-hill] for the 360-degree view, then the [New][new-calton] and [Old Calton][old-calton] burial grounds.
- [Surgeons' Hall][surgeons-hall], for centuries of tools and the Hall of Pathology.
- [Greyfriars][greyfriars] and a wander around Edinburgh's Old Town.
- Four days touring Islay with Morag, including the distilleries and Kildalton Cross.

The [Fringe][fringe] was a lot of fun. *[Earnest?][earnest]* was probably the best show we saw there: the actor playing Earnest fails to turn up, so they pull a “volunteer” out of the audience. Hilarity ensues. *A Parody of the Rings* skipped most of the last two films for time. *Knightclub* was like Fight Club meets A Knight's Tale on Monty Python's budget. Very silly.

We also saw our first adult panto, a rock-and-roll magician who picked me for a card trick, Anna Hale's funny musical *Control Freak*, *Gummy Bear War*, *Married Without Children*, *A Covert Affair*, *Werewolf Gameshow*, *How to Become a Movie Star?*, and *Girl Pop*. That last one had nine people in the audience.

### London

- [Shakespeare's Globe][globe]: *The Merry Wives of Windsor* in the afternoon and *Twelfth Night* in the evening.
- [Highgate Cemetery][highgate], both East and West, with Douglas Adams, pens, and animal monuments.
- *[My Neighbor Totoro][totoro]*: amazing puppetry. Such a good show.
- [Tate Modern][tate], where the Rothko room was still overwhelming. A child called an artwork “paint”; told it was fabric, he thought for a second and said, “Cheater.”
- Paddington bears at the station, the shop, and Leicester Square, plus Mr. Bean.
- [The Paddington Bear Experience][paddington]. Maybe aimed at a younger crowd than us, but still a hoot. We helped save Marmalade Day. Thank god we were there.
- Portobello Road, cameras for sale, old Paddington illustrations, and [Saucy Books][saucy].
