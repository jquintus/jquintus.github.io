Breakfast at the hotel was a full English for me and porridge for Ksenia. At [The Photographers' Gallery](https://thephotographersgallery.org.uk/), we saw [Zofia Rydet's](https://thephotographersgallery.org.uk/2025-photographers-gallery) portraits of Polish people and their homes, Boris Mikhailov's dark, anti-Soviet photography, and a show celebrating a hundred years of photo booths. The Mikhailov gallery was set to *The Dark Side of the Moon*. I enjoyed that bit enough that Ksenia may have had to pull me away. Ksenia did **not** buy film: sad panda. The café's chai was great, and the woman making drinks was nice.

We stopped at [Word on the Water](https://www.wordonthewater.co.uk/about-us), the bookstore on a barge beside the canal. Houseboats lined the rest of the water. In Covent Garden we found a French tea shop Ksenia liked; she bought tea for herself and friends. We wandered past shops and some street magic, stopped at a music shop, and had drinks at [Mr Fogg's](https://www.mr-foggs.com/our-locations/).

Part of our November 2025 London trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2025/2025-11-13-london-nov-2025-trip-summary %}).
