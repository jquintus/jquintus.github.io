Ksenia and I arrived on Prince Edward Island July 26 and stayed through August 2, with more oysters, lobster rolls, and Anne of Green Gables than I can count. We crossed back into New Brunswick on August 3 and spent our last two days at the Bay of Fundy. The [Raspberry Point Shuck & Sea tour](https://raspberrypoint.com/tours/) was the big oyster day: I must have eaten about 50, including some pulled straight from the water. We also got seals, sea caves, a lot of bookstores, and a five-star gas-station-adjacent food stop.

## Where we stayed

We began with a night at Elena and Gary’s in Biddeford, Maine. On PEI we stayed at [Kindred Spirits Inn & Cottages](https://www.kindredspirits.ca/) in Cavendish. Verna was a treat and filled us in on all the Anne things. On August 3 we crossed back to New Brunswick and stayed at [Broadleaf Ranch](https://broadleafranch.ca/); the house even came with a history lesson about its builder.

## Places and experiences

On PEI, we toured [Green Gables Heritage Place](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/pe/greengables), walked Lover’s Lane and the Haunted Wood Trail, visited the [Anne of Green Gables Museum](https://www.annemuseum.com/) and Montgomery’s [Cavendish historic site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/pe/greengables/cavendish), and made repeated bookstore and chocolate stops. We saw seals, cormorants, bald eagles, and a blue heron on a [boat trip from Peake’s Wharf](https://www.discovercharlottetown.com/listings/peakes-wharf-boat-tours/). The [Raspberry Point Shuck & Sea tour](https://raspberrypoint.com/tours/) took us through the oyster beds and put us to work shucking. Thunder Cove Beach gave us warm water and singing sand, though no Teacup Rock.

At the end, [Hopewell Rocks](https://www.thehopewellrocks.ca/) at low tide had foot-swallowing mud and amazing formations. The walk to [Dickson Falls](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nb/fundy/activ/sentiers-trails/dickson) was stunning. We explored the [St. Martins Sea Caves](https://www.discoversaintjohn.com/place/st-martins-sea-caves) on foot and then returned in a kayak, with Ksenia steering from the back.
