We slept at Elena and Gary’s in Biddeford, Maine, then headed north at 8 a.m. By noon we had reached [Airline Brewing Company](https://www.airlinebrewing.com/) in Amherst for lunch: a burger for me and scrambled eggs for Ksenia. We crossed the border at 2 p.m. and checked into [Kindred Spirits Inn & Cottages](https://www.kindredspirits.ca/) in Cavendish at 7. Verna was a treat and told us all about the Anne of Green Gables things to see nearby.

We walked to dinner at [The Lost Anchor](https://www.lostanchorpei.com/). The fish chowder was full of fish and lobster; we both had mussels. Tiramisu cheesecake and carrot cake cheesecake were both excellent.
