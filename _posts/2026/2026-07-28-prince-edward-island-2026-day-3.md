We had breakfast at the hotel, then drove to Charlottetown. After the rain cleared enough for parking, we conquered the restrooms at [Founders’ Food Hall](https://foundersfoodhall.com/). We saw *Anne of Green Gables: The Musical*. Matthew died with no warning, a betrayal. We walked Anne of Green Gables shops, stopped at a local craft store and photography center, then returned to the food hall for a quick lunch: a California roll burrito for Ksenia and a prosciutto sandwich for me.

Dinner was an early one at [Slaymaker & Nichols](https://slaymaker.ca/). We shared Let’s Go Mushroom Hunting and PEI mussels. Ksenia had shrimp scampi; I had a BLT with lobster. Then we found a bookstore: seven pens, four books, stickers, woodworking books, a werewolf picture book, and even a comic-book shop with a future actor. We finished with [Cows](https://cows.ca/) ice cream, blueberry for Ksenia and strawberry for me.
