We mailed postcards, then stopped at Avonlea Village, a strange shopping center transplanted from L. M. Montgomery’s school. We drove by Cactus Pot for a beach view and Fin Rocks, Covehead Harbour Lighthouse, and Dalvay by the Sea. Lunch at [Carr’s Oyster Bar](https://carrspei.ca/) was fish and chips for Ksenia, seafood chowder and a lobster roll for me, and strawberry champagne cheesecake for dessert.

The Anne trail continued with the [Anne of Green Gables Museum](https://www.annemuseum.com/), at the place that inspired the Lake of Shining Waters. We also stopped at French River Viewpoint and New London Range Rear Lighthouse, with a drink at the old Kensington train station. Dinner at the [Malpeque Oyster Barn](https://www.tourismpei.com/attractions/malpeque-oyster-barn) meant an oyster po’ boy for Ksenia and oyster chowder for me. I did not like her po’ boy.
