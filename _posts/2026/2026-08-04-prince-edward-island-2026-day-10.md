I woke to the smell of bacon. Home-cooked bacon and eggs started the day before [Hopewell Rocks](https://www.thehopewellrocks.ca/) at low tide. The mud was fun and would swallow your foot, the water was brown from the silt, and the rock formations were amazing.

At [Kelly’s Bake Shop](https://www.findglocal.com/CA/Alma/220118754683355/Kelly%27s-Bake-Shop) in Alma, I had a sticky bun while Ksenia had cookies. We walked to [Dickson Falls](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/nb/fundy/activ/sentiers-trails/dickson) through the woods, over bridges and boardwalks; it was stunning. Driving through Fundy National Park unexpectedly cost $20. Dinner at [The Caves Restaurant](https://www.cavesrestaurant.com/) in St. Martins was counter-style seafood on the water, with a great view and good food: fish and chips for Ksenia, a lobster roll for me. Dessert was ice cream at [Octopus](https://www.octopusicecream.com/).
