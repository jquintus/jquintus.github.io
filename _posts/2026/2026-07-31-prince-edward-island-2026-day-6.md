Laundry day. We had lunch at [Blue Mussel Cafe](https://bluemusselcafe.com/): six oysters, mussels to share, and lobster rolls. Then we followed a fermentation trail. At [Island Honey Wine Company](https://www.islandhoneywine.ca/) we tasted mead, cider, dry oyster cider, a Haskap honeyberry drink that was more like a red wine and drier than mead, and a peach-flavored sparkling drink. At [Deep Roots Distillery](https://www.deeprootsdistillery.com/) the tasting list included gin, spice apple, berry liqueur, and raspberry. We also stopped at a winery.

The day’s drinks deserved their own page.
