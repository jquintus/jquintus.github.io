After breakfast at the hotel, we drove to the [St. Martins Sea Caves](https://www.discoversaintjohn.com/place/st-martins-sea-caves) and walked through small streams in the gray sand. We climbed rocks, then drove up to another path through woods above the caves. Lunch at [Spinney’s Seafood Market](https://www.stmartinscanada.com/post/spinney-s-seafood-market) was lobster rolls; a couple from West Virginia sat with us.

We went to Quaco Lighthouse, then kayaked around the caves we had explored earlier. Ksenia steered from the back. She tried hard and improved! We had dinner at the hotel.
