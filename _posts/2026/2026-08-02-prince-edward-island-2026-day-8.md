We walked the [L. M. Montgomery Cavendish National Historic Site](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/pe/greengables/cavendish): the Haunted Wood Trail, Montgomery’s grave, and the site of the grandparents’ house where she grew up. There was raspberry cordial, too. We had a light lunch with a nice view at [On the Dock](https://onthedockeatery.com/) in North Rustico: oysters and mussels. Then we drove to the [Tide & Tales bookstore and cafe](https://tideandtales.ca/), had a drink, walked the shore, and hung out in a gazebo.

Back at the hotel we played cards. Dinner at [North Shore Canteen](https://www.northshorecanteen.com/) was family-style, with beans, steak tartare, mussels, quahogs, tuna crudo, sea bass, hogget, halibut, and hanger steak.
