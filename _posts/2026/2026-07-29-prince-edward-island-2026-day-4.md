We took a [seal and lobster boat tour from Peake’s Wharf](https://www.discovercharlottetown.com/listings/peakes-wharf-boat-tours/). On the way to Governors Island we watched seals, cormorants, bald eagles, and a blue heron, and hauled up a lobster trap on the way back.

For lunch at [Dave’s Lobster](https://www.daveslobster.com/), Ksenia had a seafood roll and I split my lobster roll between the “local” Maine-style and “fancy” Connecticut-style versions. We wandered Charlottetown and got ice cream at [Cows](https://cows.ca/). A church we passed was closed, so nobody got down on their knees. We bought Anne’s chocolates and returned to [The Lost Anchor](https://www.lostanchorpei.com/) for dinner: mussels, shrimp, seafood chowder, a lobster roll, and tiramisu cheesecake.
