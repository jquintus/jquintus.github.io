We had sausage and cheese at the apartment before going out in search of morning crêpes and a walking tour. Our guide, Jérôme, took us along Cours Mirabeau and through the “city of a thousand fountains”—more like 130, he said. We saw the Four Dolphins fountain, Hôtel Maurel de Pontevès, the warm moss-covered fountain, Place d'Albertas, City Hall, and the buildings around [Saint-Sauveur Cathedral](https://travel.aixenprovencetourism.com/fr/fiche/cathedrale-saint-sauveur-5538432/). The courtyard at Place d'Albertas was made a little deeper to make it look nicer. Back at the cathedral, we could see how it had absorbed an older church, which in turn had incorporated a Roman temple.

[Le Coquet](https://lecoquet-aix.eatbu.com/) was out of mussels and lemonade at lunch. Ksenia had foie gras and a mojito; I had grilled octopus. We bought soap, candy, and wine, returned to the cathedral, then took a classic French siesta at the apartment.

After another wander, dinner was at [Jardin Mazarin](https://www.jardinmazarin.com/restaurant/) by the Four Dolphins fountain. Ksenia ordered truffle scrambled eggs and duck breast in cherry sauce. I had foie gras and kangaroo. We shared white wine and a peach dessert.

Part of our August–September 2019 trip through Provence and Paris. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2019/2019-09-07-paris-2019-trip-summary %}).
