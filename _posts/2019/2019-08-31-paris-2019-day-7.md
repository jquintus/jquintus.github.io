We had breakfast at the market stalls. Ksenia got an apple-caramel crêpe; I got a savory one with egg, cheese, and sausage. I learned that I do **not** like andouillette. It's the guts.

At [Hôtel de Caumont](https://www.caumont-centredart.com/), an old mansion was showing the Guggenheim's Thannhauser collection of Impressionist art. Afterward Ksenia went wandering and shopping while I went home to make dinner.

The meal was OK. The two-burner induction stove was not. The burners were so close together that I could only fit one pan at a time, and each had small hot spots that made searing meat impossible. A pan overhung the controls, so the buttons were hard to reach and got nearly too hot to touch. There weren't even knobs. To turn a burner off, I had to select it and then press through the settings. I wouldn't cook meat on that stove again. Dinner was tuna steak with white-wine gravy, fingerling potatoes with garlic and herbs de Provence, and garlic green beans.

Part of our August–September 2019 trip through Provence and Paris. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2019/2019-09-07-paris-2019-trip-summary %}).
