We checked out of the hotel and drove to Cannon Beach to see [Haystack Rock](https://www.ci.cannon-beach.or.us/community/page/haystack-rock), “the *Goonies* never say die rock.” Lunch was at [The Wayfarer](https://www.wayfarer-restaurant.com/), just off the beach: fish and chips for Ksenia, local salmon and clam chowder for me, and a Public Coast beer. The beer was OK.

Then we drove south on Route 101. Blue water, very green country, and ridiculously tall trees. We checked into [Le Puy, a Wine Valley Inn](https://www.thesettinginn.com/willamette/) near Newberg. Dinner was at [The Dundee Bistro](https://www.dundeebistro.com/): an anniversary Pinot for me, shrimp pasta for Ksenia.

Part of our June 2019 Pacific Northwest trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2019/2019-06-10-pacific-northwest-trip-summary %}).
