We started at the [Oregon Zoo](https://www.oregonzoo.org/). The animal roll call was long: black bear, bald eagle, comb-over condor, red panda, utterly playful otters, elephants, lions and tigers and bears (oh my!), chimpanzees, penguins, goats, house cats, long-necked giraffes, and a couple of “nope nope nopes.” Lunch was fried potatoes called *papas verdes* at the zoo. Quite tasty.

After buying all the bear shirts from the gift shop—and being called out for it by the clerk—we drove to a vintage/antique shop across the river called [Urbanite](https://www.urbanitepdx.com/). Clothes and housewares were procured. We stopped at [Schilling Cider House](https://schillingcider.com/cider-houses/portland/) so Ksenia could get a flight of cider and I could get tots. Later, we waited off my wife’s buzz by going to [Jacobsen Salt Co.](https://jacobsensalt.com/). Many little jars of salt and honey came home with us.

Dinner at [St. Jack](https://stjackpdx.com/info): Ksenia had cider, foie gras, and ramp-and-asparagus pasta with peas. I had cocktails, oysters, and bone marrow. The bone marrow was rather oily. Dessert was [Salt & Straw](https://saltandstraw.com/pages/locations) ice cream: lavender and honey for Ksenia; “Berries, Beans, and BBQ Sauce” for me, with raspberry sorbet, baked-bean ganache, and strawberry BBQ sauce. Then drinks at [Breakside Brewery](https://breakside.com/).

Part of our June 2019 Pacific Northwest trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2019/2019-06-10-pacific-northwest-trip-summary %}).
