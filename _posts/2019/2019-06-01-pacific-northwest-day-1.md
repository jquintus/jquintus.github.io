We landed in Portland and picked up our rental car. At [The Inn at Northrup Station](https://www.northrupstation.com/) we dropped our bags, then took the train into the center of the city.

Lunch at [Lardo](https://www.lardosandwiches.com/locations): Ksenia had a pork meatball bánh mì and a lavender-honey cider. I had the Sancho, a brisket grilled-cheese sandwich, and a honey beer. At [Powell’s City of Books](https://www.powells.com/), Ksenia browsed animal books while I headed for computer programming, science, engineering, and electronics. We did not have to lug all the books back: shipping was an option.

[Blue Star Donuts](https://bluestardonuts.com/pages/locations) supplied an O.G. for Ksenia and a raspberry-rosemary doughnut for me. On the long walk back, we stopped at [Safeway](https://www.safeway.com/) for snacks, including a Marionberry cider from Incline Cider and Deschutes Fresh Haze IPA. We each had a can on the rooftop deck of the hotel. It had been a long day, from 4 a.m. Eastern to 9 p.m. Pacific.

Part of our June 2019 Pacific Northwest trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2019/2019-06-10-pacific-northwest-trip-summary %}).
