Portland gave us books, doughnuts, beer, and a garden. Then we went down the Oregon coast and into wine country before driving north through Mount St. Helens, Olympia, Tenino, Tacoma, Gig Harbor, and Seattle. Nine days, two states, four wineries, and three wildlife stops: the Oregon Zoo, Wolf Haven, and Point Defiance Zoo & Aquarium. I saw many wolves at Wolf Haven, but I didn't keep an animal count.

## Where We Stayed

In Portland: [The Inn at Northrup Station](https://www.northrupstation.com/), with a rooftop deck where we drank our first-night cider and beer. In wine country: Le Puy, a Wine Valley Inn, near Newberg (now [The Setting Inn Willamette Valley](https://www.thesettinginn.com/willamette/)). In Olympia: a B&B. We stayed with family and friends in Seattle.

## Places That Stuck With Me

[Powell’s City of Books](https://www.powells.com/), [Portland Art Museum](https://portlandartmuseum.org/), and [Lan Su Chinese Garden](https://lansugarden.org/visit/) made for a very Portland beginning. I liked the calm garden tea break, and the repeated trips to Powell’s were inevitable.

[Haystack Rock](https://www.ci.cannon-beach.or.us/community/page/haystack-rock) and the green drive down the coast led us to wine country. [Mount St. Helens](https://www.mshinstitute.org/visit) was cold, clouded over, and then suddenly clear enough for great views. [Wolf Haven International](https://www.wolfhaven.org/) was a highlight: many wolves. We also visited the [Oregon Zoo](https://www.oregonzoo.org/) and [Point Defiance Zoo & Aquarium](https://www.pdza.org/), then finished in Seattle at [Ada’s Technical Books](https://www.discovery.adasbooks.com/) and [Volunteer Park](https://www.seattle.gov/parks/allparks/volunteer-park).
