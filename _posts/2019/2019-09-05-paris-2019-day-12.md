[Atelier des Lumières](https://www.atelier-lumieres.com/fr) is a huge space where images are projected onto every wall. We saw Dreamed Japan, [*Verse*](https://www.atelier-lumieres.com/en/verse), and Van Gogh. Very cool; I would recommend it.

Lunch was at [Square Gardette](https://www.timeout.fr/paris/restaurants/square-gardette). I had onglet of beef and wine; Ksenia had white fish and an Orangina. We walked around [Père Lachaise Cemetery](https://www.paris.fr/lieux/cimetiere-du-pere-lachaise-4080) and saw the graves of Jim Morrison, Abélard and Héloïse, Chopin, Pissarro, and others.

For dinner at [Au Bourguignon du Marais](https://aubourguignondumarais.fr/), Ksenia had escargot and cod. I had foie gras and beef bourguignon.

Part of our August–September 2019 trip through Provence and Paris. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2019/2019-09-07-paris-2019-trip-summary %}).
