Today's cooking tour began at the market and [Château Henri Bonnaud](https://www.chateau-henri-bonnaud.fr/en/). We tasted the dry white Terre Promise, a 2018 white Palette that had spent time in oak, a 2018 Palette rosé, and a 2016 red Palette. The red was really good; Ksenia would like it.

Then we cooked. Ksenia chopped orange potatoes, carrots, basil, beets, and parsley. We made a beet salad, charred red peppers, tapenade, and rocket salad, with soup as the main course.

Dinner at [Café le Verdun](https://annuaire-entreprises.data.gouv.fr/entreprise/389708793) was mussels. They were OK, but kind of dry.

Part of our August–September 2019 trip through Provence and Paris. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2019/2019-09-07-paris-2019-trip-summary %}).
