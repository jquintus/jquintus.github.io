We drove for hours to [Johnston Ridge Observatory at Mount St. Helens](https://www.mshinstitute.org/visit). It was about 40°F, but warm in the sun and cold in the breeze. Low clouds moved off after a few minutes and gave us great views. We hiked around the volcano for about an hour. We learned about the 1980 eruption, the mountain’s enormous north-side bulge, and how quickly plant and tree life started to return.

We checked into our B&B in Olympia, then went across the road to [La Petite Maison](https://lapetitemaisonrestaurant.com/) for dinner. Ksenia had a Moscow Mule, pork belly confit, and duck confit. I had French onion soup and steak; we also had a crème brûlée. The soup earned a star in my notebook.

Part of our June 2019 Pacific Northwest trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2019/2019-06-10-pacific-northwest-trip-summary %}).
