Thirteen days in France: ten based in Aix-en-Provence, then three in Paris. We ate a lot of duck, foie gras, crêpes, and pastries; tasted wine around Bandol, Cassis, and Aix; and packed in Roman ruins, Van Gogh, Cézanne, Picasso, and one very stubborn induction stove.

The unnamed boulangerie breakfasts in Paris were a bargain: two of us ate for less than one hotel breakfast.

## Where We Stayed

We made an Airbnb in Aix-en-Provence our home base for Provence. It had odd art, a terrace, and an induction stove I never want to cook meat on again. In Paris, we stayed at a hotel; breakfast down the street was a much better deal than eating there.

## Wine and Tours

Fabien took us through [Domaines Bunan](https://bunan.com/) in Bandol and [Cassis Bodin](https://www.cassisbodin.com/) on [Day 4]({% post_url 2019/2019-08-28-paris-2019-day-4 %}). The Bodin white was mineral and flinty. We tasted wines at [Château Henri Bonnaud](https://www.chateau-henri-bonnaud.fr/en/) during a market-and-cooking tour on [Day 5]({% post_url 2019/2019-08-29-paris-2019-day-5 %}); the 2016 red Palette was really good. Pierre took us through Saint-Rémy, Les Baux, and Arles on [Day 9]({% post_url 2019/2019-09-02-paris-2019-day-9 %}).

## Other Places We Saw

Around Aix, we explored [Saint-Sauveur Cathedral](https://travel.aixenprovencetourism.com/fr/fiche/cathedrale-saint-sauveur-5538432/), [Hôtel de Caumont](https://www.caumont-centredart.com/), [Musée Granet](https://www.museegranet-aixenprovence.fr/), and Cézanne's grave at [Saint-Pierre Cemetery](https://www.aixenprovencetourism.com/fr/fiche/cimetiere-saint-pierre-5538538/). In Avignon, we toured the [Palais des Papes](https://palais-des-papes.com/) and the [Pont d'Avignon](https://avignon-pont.com/), which I still think is the least bridge I've ever seen. [Saint-Paul-de-Mausole](https://www.saintpauldemausole.fr/van-gogh.html), [Les Baux-de-Provence](https://www.lesbauxdeprovence.com/), and Arles's [Roman sites](https://www.arlestourisme.com/fr/%C3%A9poque-romaine.html) filled our day trip.

In Paris, the stained glass of [Sainte-Chapelle](https://www.sainte-chapelle.fr/) was magnificent. We also visited [Atelier des Lumières](https://www.atelier-lumieres.com/fr), [Père Lachaise](https://www.paris.fr/lieux/cimetiere-du-pere-lachaise-4080), and the [Picasso Museum](https://www.museepicassoparis.fr/fr/). We walked around [Notre-Dame](https://www.notredamedeparis.fr/en/) after the fire, and finished with the [Eiffel Tower](https://www.toureiffel.paris/en) and [Louvre](https://www.louvre.fr/en) courtyard.
