Breakfast at our B&B meant frittata with sausage and goat cheese, yogurt with a Marionberry compote, and a conversation with another guest, a meteorologist named Kelly. Then: wolves! We visited [Wolf Haven International](https://www.wolfhaven.org/) in Tenino and saw many wolves.

We stopped at [Wendy’s](https://www.wendys.com/) for lunch and at Target to replace my ripped PJs. I stayed with Agatha, Cyril, Felix, and Ziggy. Dinner was at [Reckless Noodle House](https://order.toasttab.com/online/reckless-noodle-house-seattle-2519-s-jackson-st) in Seattle. Ksenia and I both had five-spice duck confit. We enjoyed it, though it was not spicy.

Part of our June 2019 Pacific Northwest trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2019/2019-06-10-pacific-northwest-trip-summary %}).
