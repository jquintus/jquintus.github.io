At the [Portland Art Museum](https://portlandartmuseum.org/), I jotted down a whole page of names and paintings, from Georges de La Tour and John Singer Sargent to Renoir, Monet, Degas, and Van Gogh.

We had lunch at the [Deschutes Brewery Portland Public House](https://deschutesbrewery.com/pages/portland-public-house). Ksenia had Wayfarer cider. I tried a flight of six beers, including Liquid Pie Sour, Fresh Squeezed, Lil’ Squeezy Juicy, Botanic Ale, Head of the Table, and Those Were the Haze. Lunch was a B&T for me—bacon, tomato, and rosemary—with fries, plus an IPA pretzel and mussels. We took a streetcar part of the way there.

At [Lan Su Chinese Garden](https://lansugarden.org/visit/) we took a tea break with a view of the garden. I had lavender tea and Ksenia had Assam. I also enjoyed pear sake. Then we walked by the river. We wound up on the wrong side of a long carnival, walked all the way around it, and made our way back to Powell’s. We wandered the stacks again, more aimlessly this time.

Dinner was at [Clyde Common](https://pdx.eater.com/venue/1845/clyde-common): mussels and salt cod fritters for Ksenia; a burger with truffle cheddar and duck confit for me. It was wicked good. The truffle was lost, but the confit stood out. Kind of like bacon on a good bacon burger.

Part of our June 2019 Pacific Northwest trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2019/2019-06-10-pacific-northwest-trip-summary %}).
