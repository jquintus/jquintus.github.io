We went wine tasting in the Willamette Valley. At [Erath](https://www.erath.com/about/), we tried Pinots, Chardonnay, and a rosé. The 2014 Pinot Noir with blackberry notes was a standout: it had been the hottest year on record and tasted very fruity. The 2015 Willakia Chardonnay was fruity and dry.

Then [Archery Summit](https://www.archerysummit.com/visit/), where we tasted wines from different vineyards and talked about the local grapes. The 2017 Renegade Ridge had toasted marshmallow notes, and the 2017 Archer’s Edge Vineyard reminded me a little of French Burgundy. We also tried the 2016 Looney Vineyard, Arcus Vineyard, Red Hills Vineyard, and Summit Vineyard. The Summit Vineyard was my ideal Pinot.

[Colene Clemens](https://coleneclemens.com/) was next:

| Wine | What I thought |
| --- | --- |
| 2018 Rosé of Pinot | Clean, crisp, tart. |
| 2017 Chardonnay | Clean and very nice. |
| 2015 Margo Pinot | — |
| 2015 Adriane Pinot | — |
| 2015 Victoria Pinot | — |
| 2012 Adriane Pinot | — |

Finally, at [Fairsing](https://www.fairsingvineyard.com/) we tried a 2018 rosé with floral notes and “strawberry lemonade for adults,” a 2016 Chardonnay, a 2017 Chardonnay, and a nicely balanced 2016 Pinot Noir. We bought a rosé for Agatha, a rosé for Cyril and Fred, and a 2016 Chardonnay for Danielle.

Dinner at [Thistle](https://thistleisrad.com/visit-us) in McMinnville was a daily menu from farm to table. Ksenia had a gin cocktail and gnocchi with walnut nettle; I had rabbit. We split bread, fried steak tartare, smoked rockfish, and a panna cotta with rose.

Part of our June 2019 Pacific Northwest trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2019/2019-06-10-pacific-northwest-trip-summary %}).
