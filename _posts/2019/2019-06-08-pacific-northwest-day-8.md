We went to a local park with Cyril and Felix, then had breakfast at [Super Six](https://www.foodnetwork.com/restaurants/wa/seattle/super-six-restaurant): Ksenia had a Spam dish. At [Point Defiance Zoo & Aquarium](https://www.pdza.org/) in Tacoma, we saw polar bears, sharks, leopards, tigers, an Asian elephant, walrus, meerkats, and goats that we could pet. We went through the Pacific Seas Aquarium too.

We drove across the Tacoma Narrows Bridge. It didn’t move. Dinner at [Gertie and the Giant Octopus](https://www.thenewstribune.com/entertainment/restaurants/tnt-diner/article262366822.html) in Gig Harbor was tapas: not bad, especially for a mall restaurant. We ended the day with an episode of *Good Omens*.

Part of our June 2019 Pacific Northwest trip. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2019/2019-06-10-pacific-northwest-trip-summary %}).
