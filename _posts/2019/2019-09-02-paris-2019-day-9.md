We took a day trip with Pierre to Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, Les Baux, and Arles. Saint-Rémy was small and cute, with two main streets to wander. We grabbed a baguette and a croissant, then visited [Saint-Paul-de-Mausole](https://www.saintpauldemausole.fr/van-gogh.html), the hospital where Van Gogh stayed and worked. Across the road stood a Roman mausoleum and triumphal arch built about 2,000 years ago.

We stopped for crêpes and a walk in [Les Baux-de-Provence](https://www.lesbauxdeprovence.com/). I had La Savoyarde, with bacon, cheese, potatoes, and onions; Ksenia had an andouille crêpe. The village was a fortified town perched above some very nice views.

In Arles, the [Roman amphitheater and theater](https://www.arlestourisme.com/fr/%C3%A9poque-romaine.html) are both still used. Arles has French bull races, where competitors try to snatch a ribbon from the bull's horns—kind of like a golden finch, but less stabby than the Spanish version. I liked the maze of masonry in the town hall's ceiling. We also saw [Saint-Trophime](https://www.arlestourisme.com/fr/d%C3%A9tails.html?=Clo%C3%AEtre+Saint+Trophime&ident=5538418), Le Café Van Gogh, and the site where Van Gogh painted another garden.

Dinner back in Aix was at [Marie-Georgette](https://aix-en-provence.love-spots.com/adresses/manger/restaurants-aix/687-marie-georgette.html), a fancy hamburger place. It was rather bland. My burger was lightly cooked and kind of cold; I should have ordered it medium-well instead of medium-rare. There just wasn't much flavor.

Part of our August–September 2019 trip through Provence and Paris. [Trip summary]({% post_url 2019/2019-09-07-paris-2019-trip-summary %}).
